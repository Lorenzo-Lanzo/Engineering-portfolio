ABOUT / THE PERSON BEHIND THE BUILDS

Curious by nature.
Hands-on by choice.

I’m Lorenzo Lanzo, a biomedical engineer and master’s degree candidate at Politecnico di Milano, focused on electronics, embedded systems, and audio processing.

Biomedical engineer meets audio electronics.

My work connects PCB design and embedded firmware in two fields: systems for biomedical applications, and instruments for making and processing sound.

At TechRes Lab, my master’s thesis involved the development of an isolated bioelectrical acquisition system, the redesign of a ventilator control board, and their preclinical validation on lambs in collaboration with the University of Utah, where I spent a month as a guest. In my free time, I develop audio DSP modules, effects-pedal electronics, and modular synthesizer hardware.

How I work

I develop schematics and PCB layouts in KiCad and EasyEDA. Firmware is implemented with various programs depending on the embedded system. Mechanical integration is part of the process: connectors, panels, and board assemblies need to work together with the electronics.

The project pages show the available evidence for each design, from CAD assemblies to fabricated boards and experimental results.
